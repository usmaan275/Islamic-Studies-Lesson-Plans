# TEACHER EXPLANATION

## The image

### 1. كَظُلُمَاتٍ
### "Darknesses"

Not just one darkness.

**Layers of darkness.**

This can represent:

- sins
- heedlessness
- desires
- confusion
- bad influences
- losing connection with Allah

One bad decision can lead to another.

Then another.

Then another.

Until eventually the person is surrounded by darkness.

---

### 2. فِي بَحْرٍ لُّجِّيٍّ
### "In a deep sea"

Imagine being dropped into the middle of a huge ocean.

No land.

No road.

No signs.

No direction.

You don't know:

- where you came from
- where you are
- where you're going
- which direction will take you home

That is what happens when a person loses their sense of purpose.

---

### 3. يَغْشَاهُ مَوْجٌ مِّن فَوْقِهِ مَوْجٌ
### "Covered by waves upon waves"

This is especially relevant to Year 9.

One trend.

Then another trend.

Then another.

One influencer.

Then another.

One fashion.

Then another.

One opinion.

Then another.

One desire.

Then another.

The person doesn't actually know where they're going.

They're just being carried.

> **"Whatever the wave is doing, I go with it."**

---

### 4. مِّن فَوْقِهِ سَحَابٌ
### "Above it are clouds"

Even the little light that might have been available is now being blocked.

The person doesn't even stop to ask:

> "Why am I doing this?"

They just do it.

---

### 5. ظُلُمَاتٌ بَعْضُهَا فَوْقَ بَعْضٍ
### "Darkness upon darkness"

This is the frightening part.

The darkness compounds.

A person may know something is wrong.

They may even feel ashamed.

They may not want to tell:

- their parents
- their friends
- anyone

And yet they keep doing it.

Ask:

> **"If you know you don't even want people to know you're doing it... why are you doing it?"**

Pause.

Then:

> **"Because you're lost."**

> **"You're in the middle of the sea."**

> **"And instead of choosing your direction, you're following the waves."**

---

### 6. إِذَا أَخْرَجَ يَدَهُ لَمْ يَكَدْ يَرَاهَا
### "If he stretches out his hand, he can hardly see it."

This is where there is hope.

Notice:

Allah doesn't say:

> "He sees absolutely nothing."

He can **hardly** see his hand.

There is still a tiny trace of light.

That means:

> **It is not too late.**

You can still see enough to make a change.

You can still:

- fix what is broken
- find what is lost
- right the wrongs you've done
- turn back to Allah

## 1. PAST — CREATION

Ask:

> **"Can something create itself?"**

No.

> **"Can something come from absolutely nothing?"**

No.

Use the phone analogy again.

> "Imagine I put a brand-new iPhone on the table."

> "You ask me where it came from."

> "I say: nowhere."

> "It just appeared."

Would you believe me?

No.

Then:

> "Now imagine I smash the phone into thousands of pieces."

> "And I tell you those pieces randomly arranged themselves, connected themselves, created software, created a battery, created a camera and somehow produced a working phone."

Would you believe me?

Obviously not.

Then:

> "The universe is incomparably more complicated than a phone."

So we ask:

> **Who created it?**

And then the next question:

> **Who created the Creator?**

The answer is:

Allah cannot have been created.

If Allah needed another creator, that creator would be the one we are ultimately looking for.

There must ultimately be an **independent Creator** who does not depend upon anything else.

That is Allah.

---

# 2. PRESENT — WHO IS MAINTAINING YOU?

Ask:

> "Your phone needs charging."

> "Your car needs fuel."

> "Your house needs maintenance."

> "So who maintains YOU?"

Allah.

Then go through:

### Food

> "Does food guarantee that you will never be hungry?"

No.

### Water

> "Does drinking water guarantee that you will never become dehydrated?"

No.

### Sleep

> "Does sleeping for ten hours guarantee that you wake up feeling refreshed?"

No.

### The sun

> "Can the sun be shining and you still feel cold?"

Yes.

Therefore:

> **Food does not guarantee nourishment.**

> **Sleep does not guarantee rest.**

> **The sun does not guarantee warmth.**

> **These effects only occur by the command of Allah.**

> **Allah is sustaining us at every moment.**

The things we see are **means**.

Allah is the One who creates the effect.

# THE LOGICAL CONCLUSION

Tell students:

> "There are only a few logical possibilities."

Write:

### Muhammad ﷺ claimed to be a Prophet.

What are the possibilities?

**1. He was lying.**

**2. He was genuinely mistaken/deluded.**

**3. He was being influenced by something evil.**

**4. He was telling the truth.**

Now:

> "Let's go through this carefully."

---

## POSSIBILITY 1 — HE WAS LYING

Ask:

> "Was Muhammad ﷺ known for lying before Islam?"

No.

He was known as:

### الصادق الأمين
### Al-Ṣādiq al-Amīn

**The truthful, the trustworthy.**

People trusted him with:

- their possessions
- their business
- their disputes
- their belongings

Then ask:

> "Would someone known for honesty suddenly invent the biggest lie of his entire life?"

And not just tell the lie...

but spend **23 years** suffering because of it?

He was:

- mocked
- insulted
- boycotted
- attacked
- persecuted
- driven from his home

His companions were tortured.

Some were killed.

And yet he continued.

---

# POSSIBILITY 2 — HE WAS DELUDED / MISTAKEN

Ask:

> "Was his message random and inconsistent?"

No.

His message was centred around:

- worshipping one God
- morality
- justice
- mercy
- accountability
- charity
- family
- worship
- the Hereafter

For 23 years, his message remained coherent.

And it transformed an entire society.

---

# POSSIBILITY 3 — HE WAS INFLUENCED BY EVIL

Ask:

> "What kind of message did he bring?"

A message telling people to:

- worship Allah
- give charity
- be honest
- forgive
- control desires
- help the poor
- care for orphans
- establish justice
- avoid oppression
- pray
- fast
- remember Allah

Does that look like a message designed to lead people towards evil?

No.

---

# POSSIBILITY 4 — HE WAS TELLING THE TRUTH

So if:

- he wasn't lying
- he wasn't simply mistaken
- his message wasn't evil

then what remains?

> **He was telling the truth.**

> **He really was the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.**

# HIS MIRACLES

> "But character isn't the only evidence."

# MIRACLE 1 — THE MOON SPLITTING

Allah says:

> **اقْتَرَبَتِ السَّاعَةُ وَانشَقَّ الْقَمَرُ**

> **"The Hour has drawn near and the moon has split."**
>
> **54:1**

The companions also reported witnessing the event.

One narration in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī records Ibn Masʿūd رضي الله عنه saying that during the lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ, the moon was split into two parts and the Prophet ﷺ told them to bear witness. 

### Make them imagine it:

> "And the Prophet ﷺ is standing there."

# MIRACLE 2 — WATER FROM HIS FINGERS

This one is insane.

Tell them:

> "The companions are travelling."

> "They need water."

> "There isn't enough."

Then the Prophet ﷺ puts his hand into a small container.

And water begins flowing from between his fingers.

Not metaphorically.

The companions describe actually seeing water springing from between his fingers.

One narration records approximately **300 people** performing wudu from it. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Another authentic narration from Jabir رضي الله عنه describes approximately **1,500 people** drinking and performing wudu from water that flowed from between the Prophet's ﷺ fingers at al-Hudaybiyyah. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Ask:

> "How much water was originally there?"

A small amount.

Then:

> **"How many people needed it?"**

Hundreds.

Then:

> **"Where did the extra water come from?"**

Allah.

This is the point:

> **The Prophet ﷺ did not claim to be God.**

> **Allah was giving His Messenger ﷺ a miracle.**

# MIRACLE 4 — ACCEPTED DU'A

Explain:

# MIRACLE 5 — VICTORY AGAINST IMPOSSIBLE ODDS

> "They won, therefore every victory was automatically a miracle."

> **Allah gave the Prophet ﷺ extraordinary help at crucial moments.**

Examples include Badr and other events in the seerah.

Emphasise:

> "The Prophet ﷺ still planned."

> "He still took precautions."

> "He still consulted his companions."

> "He still fought."

> "Miracles did not mean he stopped using the means Allah had given him."

# KEY TAKEAWAYS

By the end of the lesson, students should understand:

### ALLAH
- Creation requires a Creator.
- Allah is independent and uncreated.
- Everything depends upon Allah.
- Food, sleep, water and the sun are means — Allah creates their effects.
- We will all eventually return to Allah.

### THE DUNYA
- The dunya can distract and deceive us.
- Following every trend can leave a person spiritually directionless.
- The Qur'an describes this as darkness upon darkness.
- Even when a person feels completely lost, there is still a way back.

### MUHAMMAD ﷺ
- He was known as **al-Ṣādiq al-Amīn** before prophethood.
- His character supports the truthfulness of his claim.
- His seerah and impact are extraordinary.
- He dealt with enemies with remarkable forgiveness.
- Allah supported him with miracles.
- The Qur'an contains prophecies connected to future events.
- His guidance gives Muslims a fixed direction rather than allowing society to carry them wherever the waves go.

### THE BIG IDEA

> **The dunya is full of waves.**

> **Islam gives us an anchor.**

> **The Prophet ﷺ gives us a model to follow.**

> **Allah gives us the light.**
